# Phase 9 Official BIR Reference Starting Points

Review these official BIR resources during implementation and again during Phase 9 validation:

- BIR Forms — official list of current forms and descriptions
- BIR Form 2551Q — Quarterly Percentage Tax Return
- BIR Form 1701Q — Quarterly Income Tax Return for applicable individuals, estates, and trusts
- BIR Tax Reminder — current filing reminders and deadlines
- eBIRForms — current supported forms and filing application information
- Secondary Registration — registration of books of accounts and authority for computerized or loose-leaf systems

## Implementation Rule

Do not copy a rate, deadline, deduction rule, or form requirement into permanent code without:

1. Recording the official source
2. Recording the effective date
3. Recording the date reviewed
4. Making the rule configurable
5. Preserving the historical rule used by completed worksheets
